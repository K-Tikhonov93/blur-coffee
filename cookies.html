Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device to make it work, remember your choices or gather information. Similar technologies (such as your browser's local storage) work in the same way. This policy explains which ones we use on this website.

2. Cookies we use

Blur is a simple, static website. We do not use advertising or analytics/tracking cookies. We only use what is strictly necessary, plus a third-party map:

NameTypePurposeDuration
blur_cookieStrictly necessary (local storage)Remembers that you have seen and accepted this cookie notice, so it is not shown again.Persistent until you clear it
blur_langStrictly necessary (local storage)Remembers your language choice (EN / ES).Persistent until you clear it
Google MapsThird-partyOur location map is embedded from Google Maps, which may set its own cookies when the map loads.Set by Google

3. Third-party cookies

The embedded map is provided by Google. When it loads, Google may set cookies under its own policies, over which we have no control. You can read more in Google's cookie policy.

4. How to manage cookies

You can accept or dismiss the notice using the banner shown when you first visit. You can also block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings — for example in Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge, under "Privacy" or "Cookies". Note that blocking the map may prevent it from displaying correctly.

5. Contact

For any question about this Cookie Policy, write to us at hello@blur.coffee.

1. ¿Qué son las cookies?

Las cookies son pequeños archivos de texto que una web guarda en tu dispositivo para funcionar, recordar tus preferencias o recoger información. Tecnologías similares (como el almacenamiento local de tu navegador) funcionan del mismo modo. Esta política explica cuáles usamos en esta web.

2. Cookies que utilizamos

Blur es una web sencilla y estática. No usamos cookies publicitarias ni de analítica/seguimiento. Solo usamos lo estrictamente necesario, además de un mapa de terceros:

El mapa incrustado lo proporciona Google. Al cargarse, Google puede instalar cookies según sus propias políticas, sobre las que no tenemos control. Puedes leer más en la política de cookies de Google.

Puedes aceptar o cerrar el aviso mediante el banner que aparece en tu primera visita. Ten en cuenta que bloquearlas puede impedir que el mapa se muestre correctamente.
